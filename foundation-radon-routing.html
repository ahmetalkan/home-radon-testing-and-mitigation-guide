Foundation Types and Radon Pipe Routing Decisions

Foundation construction determines both how soil gas is collected and how cleanly the vent pipe reaches the roofline without freezing condensation or hurting curb appeal.

Foundation typeTypical collection strategyKey site detail
Poured basement slabSub-slab suction pit(s) cored through concrete floorCompact clay sub-base may require two suction points tied to one riser
Dirt or gravel crawl spaceSub-membrane depressurization beneath sealed heavy plastic linerLiner seams taped and sealed airtight to foundation walls and piers
Slab-on-gradeSuction through garage slab, utility closet, or exterior wall coreVerify where interior heating ducts or plumbing lines sit beneath slab

Interior routing through an attached garage or closet protects the fan and pipe from freezing winter condensate, whereas exterior siding runs avoid cutting through finished upper floors. Detailed foundation planning notes appear in choosing a radon system by foundation type on RadonLab.
